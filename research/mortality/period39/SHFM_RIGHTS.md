## Domain and time origin

The landing page describes projected survival at baseline and after interventions **for patients with heart failure**, primarily for cardiac healthcare providers. It does not define baseline as the first HF diagnosis date. These pages alone therefore do not qualify a healthy-to-incident-HF-to-death bridge or a diagnosis-origin survival distribution. They provide no verified Russian calibration, nor an explicit complete40–80 age-domain contract.

The update page says the2013 revision extended application to higher-risk hospitalized patients using additional treatment/device indicators. The2017 revision moved the desktop browser implementation from Java to Javascript, added unit options and optional defaults for missing laboratory values by CHF class. Those UI defaults are not validated measurements and must not silently enter a research schema.

## Version and horizon boundaries

The inspected update history separately labels2013 and2017 changes. It states that2013 altered the0–5year baseline survival using observed1–5year data, whereas the original model extrapolated beyond its shorter observed follow-up. It also distinguishes mean lifetime expectancy from median survival. Thus original2006 and later website baselines must not be treated as an identical frozen artifact; a five-year function cannot be silently extended to10years merely because lifetime expectancy is displayed. Exact current deployed source/release identity and a complete10year contract were not established here. Footer dates are not version identifiers.
