# Detailed-cause historical experiment: analysis plan

2026-09-15. Recorded after reviewing official NHEFS1992 and NHANES I codebooks, before retrieving participant files. This is a new historical proof-of-method study, not contemporary RU/DE validation or a registered clinical trial.

## Scope and source
The original target taxonomy has20 noninfectious disease modules. Official NHEFS1992 vital/tracing and mortality files retain underlying ICD9 cause, unlike recent coarse public NHANES linkage. Baseline NHANES I1971-1975 covariates and public follow-up through1992/1993 are the only individual sources. Never use later questionnaires as baseline predictors. ICD9 families are operational definitions, not asserted exact code-by-code equivalence with ICD10.

Age25-74 at baseline; valid recorded sex; positive relevant baseline examination weight; known examination year/month and last-contact/death year/month; positive follow-up. Unknown last vital status is censored at last confirmed contact, not treated as a horizon survivor. Missing day, if needed, uses15 and is counted; missing month/year excludes the observation with an audit reason. Zero follow-up exclusions are reported. Source-imputed core laboratory values (flag not0) are not treated as observed measurements. Only complete profiles are compared, on identical people across panels.

Primary population: original nutrition/detailed locations1-65, SWSP2 weights, revised strata2/PSU2. Augmentation locations66-100 do not have serum albumin in the core laboratory file and are excluded from the primary profile. Primary clinical comparator: nonlinear age, sex-specific baseline, BMI and systolic BP. Laboratory extension: measured serum albumin, total cholesterol and hemoglobin. These are the baseline variables available in this source without a detailed-subsample restriction; this comparator is not represented as a full modern clinical score with smoking and medications.

Secondary prespecified detailed-sample analysis, only if codes and sample support permit: original detailed locations1-65 with SWDP2 weights; add baseline smoking if documented, and renal/hepatic chemistry (creatinine, BUN, AST, alkaline phosphatase, uric acid), compare on the same expanded complete profiles. Analyze separately, do not pool its metrics with primary or select only a favourable analysis. Extra source dictionaries are inspected before performance calculations; any change is documented.

## Endpoint taxonomy
Map twenty original disease families to prespecified ICD9 prefixes: IHD410-414; HF/cardiomyopathy425,428; cerebrovascular430-438; hypertension401-405; AF/flutter4273; aortic aneurysm441; chronic lower respiratory490-496; dementia/Alzheimer290,3310,3312; Parkinson332; diabetes250; renal580-589 (broad renal family, not CKD alone); chronic liver571; lung162; colorectal153-154; pancreas157; breast174-175; prostate185; liver155; stomach151; hematologic200-208. Record category definitions and source equivalence caveats. External causes800-999 in this death-certificate file have an implicit E prefix per CDC; chapter infections001-139 and pneumonia/influenza480-487 are separately retained competitors. Other/unknown deaths remain events; residual is not declared wholly noninfectious. Target-specific CIFs are valid subprobabilities even when residual causes are mixed.

Select estimable disease heads using DEVELOPMENT events only: at least10 deaths by20years within the primary development domain. Unselected rare target families remain visible in the coverage table and are pooled in the residual, not assigned zero risk and not counted as estimated heads. Goal is at least10 individually coded noninfectious families. Always publish all20 counts, selected status and the selected families ranked by development counts; this is not the official national top20.

## Split, models and tuning
Preserve clustering: split entire revised strata before model fitting, using numpy default_rng(20260915) to permute sorted unique eligible strata; hold out ceil(0.30*n_strata), develop on the remainder. Holdout has no tuning or recalibration. This is an internal held-out-design-domain test, not geographic or temporal external validation. Holdout-domain averages are not asserted national population rates.

Piecewise exponential cause-specific hazards with intervals0-5,5-10,10-20years. Sex-specific interval baselines; continuous baseline features, nonlinear age through scaled age and age squared. Winsorization0.5/99.5percentiles, means and SD learned only inside each training fold. No missing-value imputation. Coefficients are shrunk toward a same-fold fitted all-cause coefficient vector; this is penalized partial pooling, not literature-assigned coefficients or a fully Bayesian posterior. Log-baseline weak ridge0.25 keeps zero-event sex/interval cells finite. Prostate hazard is structurally zero for recorded female participants and trained only among recorded males; no analogous rule for breast cancer.

Penalty grid1,10,100 selected separately for clinical and laboratory panels by four development-strata folds and mean15year multicategory IPCW Brier. Cause selection fixed from outer development only; all folds use that same partition. Preprocessing and pooling target refitted inside each fold. Publish all CV scores, convergence diagnostics, coefficients and transformed-variable definitions. Clinical and lab fit once on full development after tuning; no selection based on heldout performance.

## Evaluation
Primary contrast: laboratory minus clinical multicategory IPCW Brier at15years for survival plus all estimated causes plus infection/external/residual. Secondary10year scores, all-cause Brier and cumulative/dynamic AUC, per-cause Brier/AUC/O-E, and sex/age25-49/50-64/65-74 descriptions. When estimating a cause-specific binary AUC, other causes of death are noncases, not censored. Report raw events and IPCW incidence separately. Reverse weighted KM for censoring estimated in the evaluation domain, deaths before censoring at ties, censoring exactly at horizon observed; preserve full survey-weight denominator. Check IPCW incidences against independent weighted Aalen-Johansen calculation.

1000 paired rescaled PSU bootstrap draws within heldout revised strata; resample units before complete-domain restriction and refit the censoring distribution per draw. Seed20260920. Report undefined draws explicitly; no nominal interval claimed after deleting failures. Intervals condition on fitted models and exclude training, tuning and source-code uncertainty. No noninferiority, causal treatment effect, modern national risk or assay-transfer claim.
