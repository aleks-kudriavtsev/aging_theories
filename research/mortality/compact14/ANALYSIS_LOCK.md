# Iteration14: compact panel, analysis lock

Date:2026-09-10. Base:a50a9062a5527fcd5cc146883a8974326ce718b6. This Git decision log precedes retrieval of NHANES2015-2016 participant outcomes; it is not a clinical trial registration.

## Fixed question and panels
Primary question: does clinical history/physiology plus a compact renal-metabolic-protein panel improve three-year four-state Brier over the existing clinical model on NHANES2015-2016?

Compact4 reported measurements: HbA1c, creatinine-derived eGFR, urinary albumin/creatinine ratio (UACR), serum albumin. All13 original clinical engineered predictors remain. The renal axis uses creatinine/eGFR and albuminuria; no substitution of sTNFR1 for TNF-alpha or assignment of literature HR as coefficients. UACR requires two underlying urine determinations: four reported measures are not four physical assays, nor a proven cost halving.

Comparators: frozen iteration12 clinical model; frozen full eight-measure no-CRP model; frozen six-measure model without RDW/WBC from the assay sensitivity analysis. No comparator coefficients are re-estimated. Compact4 alone is trained on the identical complete no-CRP development sample, NHANES1999-2010, using previous feature algebra, five cause-specific piecewise exponential hazards, intervals0-5/5-8years. Ridge penalty chosen from1,10,100 using the original three leave-calendar-block-out development folds and mean five-year six-state Brier, tie favouring larger penalty. These are internal blocked folds (not forward-only forecasting). Preprocessing is fitted inside each development fold and then on full development data. Publish compact coefficients and digest before retrieving new participant data. No evaluation-based tuning, recalibration, or automated selection of a winner.

## New evaluation population
One previously uninspected cycle2015-2016, baseline age40-79, valid sex, positive MEC weights, mortality eligible, nonnegative examination follow-up. Primary comparison uses the same complete full-eight-measure clinical/laboratory profiles for every model, avoiding different participants as explanation for performance differences. A prespecified secondary coverage analysis reports eligibility for clinical, compact4, six- and eight-measure inputs among all otherwise eligible persons; comparison on each model's own eligible population is not evidence of superiority. No imputation or silent assay substitution.

Public2015-2016 mortality outcomes have only heart diseases, malignant neoplasms and other causes. Consequently validation aggregates the five learned cause-CIFs to exactly three deaths plus survival; chronic respiratory and cerebrovascular heads are NOT validated separately. Unknown cause among known deaths remains other/unknown and is counted, never censored. All-cause includes infections/external deaths; no noninfectious-only claim. Primary horizon3years, secondary1year; ascertainment required for every survivor or stop and issue an explicit amendment, no covert removal of short-follow-up survivors. No2017-2018 retrieval in this iteration.

## Measures and uncertainty
Primary: paired difference in four-state Brier, compact4 minus clinical at3years. Secondary descriptive: full8 minus clinical, compact4 minus full8, compact4 minus six6; all-cause AUC/Brier, mean calibration bias, observed/expected and calibration slope, per-cause metrics, sex and age40-59/60-79 groups. Report all compared models, including unfavourable differences. No predefined noninferiority margin: an interval including zero is NOT equivalence or noninferiority.

Use WTMEC2YR, cycle-specific stratum and PSU.1000 paired rescaled-PSU bootstrap draws, seed20260914. All interval reports count undefined draws; intervals condition on fixed fitted models and exclude fitting/selection uncertainty. Primary complete-profile analysis is a survey domain: draw PSUs in the broader eligible sample, then restrict to the common complete domain. Identical resampled weights for comparisons. Secondary results have no multiplicity-corrected confirmatory claim. Calibration regressions are diagnostics, never fitted into the deployed prediction. Bin and subgroup results include event counts. No arbitrary clinical decision threshold.
