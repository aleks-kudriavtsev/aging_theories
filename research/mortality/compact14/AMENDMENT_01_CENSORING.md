# Amendment01: ascertainment-triggered IPCW evaluation

Date2026-09-10. The frozen coefficient commit96f0ca8 and original protocol2389c6e remain unchanged. New participant inputs were retrieved; the initial evaluation stopped at its required follow-up check BEFORE prediction metrics or comparative results were calculated. Inspection of follow-up showed3256 otherwise eligible participants,3098 alive at follow-up, four alive with examination follow-up35months (rather than the required36). This finding concerns ascertainment, not a favourable/unfavourable model comparison.

The original unweighted-binary-outcome evaluation is not executed. Primary horizon remains3years and all four planned model comparisons remain. Do not remove the four persons or label them3year survivors. Use IPCW with a survey-weighted reverse Kaplan-Meier censoring distribution, fitted in each evaluated domain; weight death by1/G(T-) and known survival by1/G(h-). With discrete month ties, deaths precede administrative censoring; censoring at the horizon counts as observed through that horizon, whereas earlier censoring has zero outcome weight. Reverse-KM censor risk sets exclude deaths tied at that instant. This convention is used consistently in independent reference checks. Assume independent censoring within the analysed domain, explicitly a methodological assumption rather than proven fact.

Four-state and binary Brier use sum(survey_weight*IPCW*loss)/sum(survey_weight), retaining the full original denominator. AUC uses IPCW-weighted case/noncase ranks. Report raw observed event counts separately from IPCW cumulative incidence; report mean predictions in the entire domain, including early-censored participants. Calibration regressions use known statuses with IPCW; they remain diagnostics, not risk-model refitting. Censor-distribution positivity must hold at the horizon; otherwise no value is emitted. One-year secondary horizon uses the same implementation and coincides with fully observed evaluation when there is no earlier censoring.

For each of1000 paired rescaled PSU draws, resample in the entire eligible survey sample, then restrict to the identical complete comparison domain and re-estimate the censoring distribution using replicate survey weights. Count undefined draws; no silent deletion. Subgroup and bin estimates re-estimate G inside their respective domains and remain descriptive.

As a nonparametric numerical check, compare IPCW cause probabilities with independently computed survey-weighted Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence, including tied times. Preserve all failed/pre-amendment checks in the audit trail. No coefficients, variable selection, inclusion criteria or horizon are changed; no performance-driven decision is made. Original compact_panel14.py is preserved as the exact training script identified in the frozen model; amended evaluation is in a separate module.

Methodological sources: Graf et al.,1999, PMID10474158; Gerds & Schumacher,2006, DOI10.1002/bimj.200610301, PMID17240660. The original pre-data lock and this post-ascertainment/pre-performance amendment must both be described; the entire final method must not be called unchanged preregistration.
