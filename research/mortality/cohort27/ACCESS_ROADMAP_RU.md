## New general contract/import gate recommendations

Access: exact cohort/release URLs, access_class public_unrestricted vs approved_controlled vs metadata_only, explicit policy/source evidence, execution environment and actor/institution country, product-specific scope, no AI restrictions silently overridden, no automatic request/login/agreement path. Policy unknown stays blocked. For controlled cohorts, operator attestation is not legally authenticated by code.

Source: local-only paths with stable fileSHA/byte-length map, pinned schema, duplicate keys/IDs rejected, explicit release and metadata locator; verify all needed source objects before parsing any rows. Public metadata source does not stand in for person-level scientific files.

Assay: source variable, analyte/fragment/UniProt, serum/plasma/urine matrix, unit/scale, assay/platform, baseline specimen/visit, LOD/LOQ/released replacement/flag policy, optional externally established bridge reference. Do not convertNPX/RFU toconcentration; do not replaceTNF withreceptor, GDF15 withproxy, orUACR withurine albumin alone. Record true missing predictors and reject altered frozen-panel applicability.

Endpoint: explicit death status and time origin/unit, followup censoring date, all-cause vs composite definition, linkage/release, ties/zero-month convention, horizon support and event counts from the actual same-person domain. Left truncation/repeated visits need a declared new design, not invisible adaptation. Separate ready_for_import from ready_for_training/validation; a pass never creates clinical readiness.

Sampling: exact sourceweights/strata/PSU/subsample eligibility and complete-case domain; extra assays often have a different subpopulation. Joint overlap/event counts must come from received admissible records, not marginal catalogue counts.

Output: audit-only aggregates plus true/false/unknown blockers; synthetic fixtures prominently markedsoftware-only. No empirical AUC/Brier if all candidate paths remain blocked. PublicNHANESIII may be a viable distinct route; assessed by another agent, not determined in this access review.
