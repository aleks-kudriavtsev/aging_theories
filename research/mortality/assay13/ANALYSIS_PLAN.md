# Assay robustness 13: fixed computational sensitivity plan

Date: 2026-09-10. Base: 95163a6f264d30c85c2d7c1d383f3b745456bb16. This plan precedes the sensitivity calculations, NOT the original observation of outcomes. NHANES2011-2014 was already examined in iteration12. This is a post-validation mechanistic stress test, not new independent validation, not an assay experiment and not an intervention effect.

Freeze the primary model (SHA256 40069d1a98a29308bee26e5dac895388f4c9008b983bc834ad8ac0a57fbcd094) and secondary no-CBC model (SHA256 7c7dc918f042ff44f141a9ad3b49e6573db7f9732c5eb9d93e29b09b0686fa24). No coefficient changes, refitting, outcome-guided correction or preferred-model selection.

Reuse exactly the complete-profile cohorts 2011-2012 (2666;5-year horizon) and2013-2014 (3013;4-year horizon). Verify all original scientific input hashes. Reproduce original baseline metrics before perturbation. Never drop a participant because a simulated result is inconvenient; stop on invalid perturbation. Recompute derived features from the perturbed raw measurement, including creatinine→eGFR and urinary albumin→UACR; freeze training transformations and clipping. Ages, sex, covariates other than the chosen measurements, weights and outcomes stay unchanged.

Grid: baseline; proportional ±5% and ±10% separately for total cholesterol, HDL, HbA1c, serum creatinine, UACR, serum albumin, RDW-CV and WBC (32); RDW-CV additive ±0.3 and ±0.5 percentage points (4); HbA1c additive ±0.2 and ±0.4 percentage points (4); albumin additive ±2 g/L (2); joint CBC shifts RDW+0.3 percentage points and WBC+5%, and both reversed (2). Total45 scenarios, both frozen laboratory models, two cycles. These perturbations are selected engineering scenarios, not observed assay biases or allowable analytical-error limits.

For every condition report six-state Brier, all-cause AUC/Brier, weighted mean risk and its shift, weighted mean/p95 absolute individual risk change, fraction with absolute change>1 percentage point (engineering threshold, not clinical decision), clipping fraction and all cause-specific weighted risk shifts. Different horizons are not pooled. All scenarios retained, including apparent improvements due to compensating pre-existing calibration error.

For four fixed sentinels (RDW+0.5pp, albumin−5%, HbA1c+0.2pp, joint CBC positive), use1000 paired rescaled-PSU bootstrap draws per cycle, seed20260913+cycle. Report conditional percentile intervals for changes in mean risk, AUC and six-state Brier; conditional on profiles, selected perturbations and fixed models, excluding training/assay-parameter uncertainty. Subgroup sensitivity: sex and age40-59/60-79 for these four sentinels; descriptive, no subgroup-driven tuning.

Implement a separate paired-method analysis tool: candidate=a+b*reference, Deming regression with explicitly supplied error-variance ratio var(candidate)/var(reference), constant-variance assumption, unique specimen IDs and disjoint fit/validation splits; raw and corrected held-out agreement, no automatic clinical acceptance. Synthetic examples remain labelled synthetic and cannot authorize application. No actual paired laboratory measurements are currently available. Do not infer a bridge from population means, risk calibration or correlation alone. Actual deployment requires a locked independent assay-comparison study and subsequent risk-model transport validation.

Export code, aggregate results, source/version manifest, unit tests and report; no participant records or specimen identities in git. Prior conference text and default Workbench stay unchanged. Scientific endpoints remain broad historical-US cause groups including competing infections/external causes; no claim of RU/DE calibration or pure noninfectious individual risk.
