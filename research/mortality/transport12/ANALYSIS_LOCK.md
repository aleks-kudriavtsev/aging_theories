# Analysis lock 12: no-CRP panel and five competing causes

Date 2026-09-10. Base: endpoint-readiness11 commit 85f9df19ea87bbe1cffd94d008af2a9acf84167d. This versioned analysis plan precedes retrieval of individual NHANES2011-2014 records in this iteration. It is not an externally registered protocol. All 1999-2010 cycles were previously inspected and may now be used for development, not relabelled independent validation.

## Fixed research question
Does a routine panel that does not require CRP improve coherent five-cause mortality predictions over the same clinical predictors on participant-disjoint later cycles? We distinguish scientific evidence for overall prediction from uncertain rare-cause and subgroup results.

## Development and evaluation
Development: eligible NHANES1999-2010 examinations, age40-79, known sex, positive appropriate MEC weights, eligible public2019 mortality linkage and nonnegative follow-up. Complete cases are defined by the no-CRP routine feature set, identically for both compared panels. No selection based on CRP, archived cardiac proteins or death cause. Missing causes of known deaths remain events in the residual group. Missing required clinical/laboratory inputs are not imputed. Exclusions and event counts are reported.

Primary new evaluation: NHANES2011-2012, fixed5year horizon. Secondary replication: NHANES2013-2014, fixed4year horizon, chosen in advance to avoid assuming five complete years of follow-up before public2019 linkage. Secondary1year outcomes in both cycles. If a survivor has less observation than the specified horizon, that fixed-horizon analysis is blocked rather than counting them as alive; any censoring-weighted replacement requires a documented amendment. Do not swap primary/secondary cycles after results. Evaluation inputs may be obtained only after model fitting, coefficient freezing and SHA256 recording. Neither later cycle is used for tuning, recalibration or winner selection.

## Outcomes
Five mutually exclusive underlying-death categories: heart diseases UCOD_LEADING001; malignant neoplasms002; chronic lower respiratory diseases003; cerebrovascular diseases005; all remaining/unknown underlying deaths. Public UCOD labels apply through2014 and are not used as fine ICD diagnoses. Heart diseases are not IHD alone; respiratory group is not COPD alone. Infections/external/unknown deaths remain in residual. Total noninfectious natural risk, detailed IHD, N18 and cancer sites are NOT identified by this partition.

## Predictors and fitting
Clinical13 features fixed from prior model: age, age_rcs, male, age_male, smoker_current, smoker_former, bmi, bmi_sq, sbp, bp_treatment, diabetes_history, cvd_history, cancer_history. Routine panel adds9 engineered features: total_cholesterol, hdl, hba1c, egfr_low, egfr_high, log_uacr, albumin, rdw, log_wbc. The two eGFR pieces are one underlying laboratory-derived measure. No log_crp or CRP placeholder. Same previously reviewed feature algebra and CDC cycle-specific conversions; any metadata-only harmonization amendments precede outcome evaluation.

Cause-specific ridge piecewise-exponential hazards with two intervals0-5 and5-8years; follow-up capped at8years. Baseline intercepts unpenalized, slopes penalized. Common penalty per panel selected from1,10,100 by three calendar-block folds: hold out1999-2002,2003-2006,2007-2010 in turn. Each fold learns preprocessing from its training part only. Score is the equal mean across heldout blocks of weighted six-state Brier at5years; exact ties prefer the larger penalty. Final fit uses all development observations. Input winsorization0.5/99.5percentiles and standardization are training-only, unweighted, preserving prior preprocessing design. Survey likelihood weights scaled to mean1. Pooled development weights: WTMEC4YR/3 for1999-2002; WTMEC2YR/6 for2003-2010. If any cause/interval cell has zero training events, stop the fit; do not add artificial deaths or borrow evaluation outcomes. Report coefficient dimensions and event support, not a binary events-per-parameter approval claim.

## Performance and uncertainty
Primary comparison: routine minus clinical six-state Brier at5years in2011-2012; lower is better. Report all six-state probabilities, binary Brier/AUC, observed/expected ratios and calibration diagnostics for each cause and all-cause death. Secondary4year replication2013-2014 is reported regardless of direction. Binary cause AUC treats competing deaths as observed noncases at the horizon. It is not a disease diagnostic sensitivity/specificity.

1000 paired rescaled-PSU bootstrap draws in each cycle, seed20260912. Resample m-1PSUs with replacement within strata, multiply by m/(m-1); survey weights remain on individuals. All comparisons in a draw use identical resampling. Percentile2.5/97.5intervals condition on fitted models and chosen penalty; no claim to training/tuning uncertainty. Keep counts of undefined rare-cause AUC draws. Prespecified descriptive groups: recorded sex, age40-59/60-79, and their intersections where counts allow. No separate subgroup refitting, no selecting best subgroup. Calibration regressions are diagnostics and do not update prediction.

## Integrity and reporting
Audit source checksums,1:1joins, identifier disjointness, censoring support, raw zero XPORT decoding, mortality-reader agreement, cause totals, monotone cumulative incidence, sum(survival,CIFs)=1, and independent numerical replay. No participant rows, identifiers or predictions enter git, public website or shared scientific archive. Source files remain local or briefly in access-controlled research artifacts.

Both favourable and unfavourable results are kept in the technical report. Any new hypothesis suggested by evaluation is explicitly post hoc and requires another validation for a revised model. Old models/default Workbench remain unchanged; no clinical, RU/DE,20year or fine-ICD enablement. Updated conference text may use achieved results with exact endpoint/population and distinguish primary from secondary analysis; acceptance is not guaranteed.
