## Scope
All seven analytes enter a typed measurement registry with identity, specimen, unit, assay and availability. No proxy substitution (BNP/NT-proBNP; IL6R/IL6; TNFR1/TNF; neurofilament heavy/light), no literature-HR multiplication, and no imputation of a protein absent from an entire cohort. NPX/RFU measurements stay platform-relative, never converted directly to pg/mL. Adding metadata is not equivalent to training a seven-protein model.
